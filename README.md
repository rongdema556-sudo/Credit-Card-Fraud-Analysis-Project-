### Credit-Card-Fraud-Analysis-Project-
## Project Title: Credit card fraud analysis 
A dynamic, interactive data vasualization tool build to tracking fraud risk which focus on user attributes 

## A short introduction 
 Credit-Card-Fraud-Analysis dashboard highlights $22.47M in total credit card fraud across 593K transactions, predominantly driven by the transportation sector, which accounts for $13.58M (60.43%) of all losses, followed distantly by health ($2.18M) and travel ($1.64M). Demographically, fraud losses skew moderately toward female cardholders ($12.73M / 56.64%) compared to males ($9.74M / 43.36%), reflecting higher fraudulent activity and volume in the female segment.

## Tech Stack 
The dashboard was build using the following tools and technologies 
- Power BI Desktop -main data visualization platform used for report creation.
- Excel -Cleaning and transform data for correct and meaningfull insight
- DAX -Used for calculation measures, dynamic visual , and conditional logic.

  ## Features / highlight
  ### Business Problem
  The financial institution is experiencing significant revenue loss from fraudulent credit card transactions, totaling $22.47M across 593K transactions. Without granular visibility into where and how fraud occurs, risk       management teams cannot effectively target fraud rules, leading to unchecked financial leakage and increased operational overhead.

  ### Goal of the Dashboard
  Deliver real-time visibility into fraud volume, total financial loss, and merchant-level trends.Break down fraudulent activity across merchant categories and cardholder demographics to identify vulnerability hotspots.

   ### Business Impact
  Intervening in the highest-risk segment (transportation) can mitigate over 60% ($13.58M) of total fraud exposure.

   ### Key insight
  we found that fraud losses are 13.3 percentage points higher on female cardholder accounts ($12.73M / 56.64%) compared to male accounts ($9.74M / 43.36%), driven in part by higher baseline transaction counts (325K vs.       268K).
